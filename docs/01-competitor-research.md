01 — Competitor Research & Market Reference

Project: AURELLE LIVING

Research reference: Danetti — https://www.danetti.com/
Target market: United Kingdom
Research type: Public website and customer-experience analysis
Project relationship: Independent research only. No affiliation with Danetti.

---

1. Research Objective

The purpose of this research is to understand how an established furniture retailer structures its online shopping experience, presents products, communicates delivery information, supports customers, and handles post-purchase processes.

The findings will inform the requirements of AURELLE LIVING, an independent luxury furniture e-commerce brand.

This research does not authorize copying Danetti's branding, website code, text, product photography, or protected creative assets.

2. Reference Website Overview

Danetti is a furniture retailer offering products for different areas of the home.

The public website demonstrates several important e-commerce capabilities:

- Product discovery and category browsing
- Furniture collections and curated product ranges
- Product-specific information
- Delivery and availability communication
- Customer support and FAQs
- Order and return support
- Payment information
- Trade customer enquiries

3. Product Discovery

Observed patterns

The reference website organizes furniture into product ranges and styles. Its public sofa pages also demonstrate collection names and style-based discovery.

Requirements for AURELLE LIVING

- Dedicated furniture category pages
- Curated collections
- Product search
- Product filtering and sorting
- Product availability indicators
- Related product recommendations
- Clear category navigation

Proposed categories

- Living Room
- Dining Room
- Bedroom
- Home Office
- Lighting
- Rugs & Accessories
- Storage

These are proposed AURELLE LIVING categories, not a claim that they exactly reproduce Danetti's catalogue.

4. Product Detail Experience

Research observations

Furniture product pages need to communicate more than a product name and price. Customers need sufficient information to assess dimensions, materials, finish, availability, and delivery expectations.

Requirements

Each AURELLE LIVING product page should support:

- Product name and SKU
- Price and sale price, where applicable
- Multiple product images
- Product description
- Dimensions and weight
- Material and finish
- Available variants
- Stock or lead-time information
- Delivery information
- Assembly information
- Care instructions
- Customer reviews, when genuine reviews exist
- Related products

No fabricated customer reviews or product specifications will be published.

5. Delivery Experience

Verified reference observations

Danetti publishes delivery information explaining:

- Furniture delivery arrangements
- Delivery date selection for eligible orders
- Two-hour delivery time notifications
- Tracking information
- Two-person furniture delivery
- Different handling for furniture and accessories

Reference: https://www.danetti.com/pages/delivery

Requirements for AURELLE LIVING

- Delivery policy page
- Delivery estimate communication
- Delivery charge calculation or clearly disclosed fixed charges
- Order status information
- Tracking integration only when a real provider is connected
- Clear communication for delayed or unavailable products

Actual UK shipping operations, delivery partners, charges, and service commitments must be established before accepting real orders.

6. Returns and Customer Support

Verified reference observations

Danetti's FAQ explains a customer portal-based returns process, return conditions, damaged-product reporting, and refund timelines.

Reference: https://www.danetti.com/pages/faqs

Requirements for AURELLE LIVING

- Returns policy
- Customer support contact page
- Return request workflow
- Damaged item reporting
- Refund status communication
- Order lookup and customer verification
- Frequently asked questions

All policies must be written for AURELLE LIVING's actual operations and reviewed for applicable UK consumer law.

7. Payment Experience

Verified reference observations

Danetti's FAQ lists card payments, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, alongside a finance option subject to eligibility.

Reference: https://www.danetti.com/pages/faqs

Requirements for AURELLE LIVING

- Secure payment-provider integration
- Clear order summary
- Shipping and tax calculation
- Payment status verification
- Order confirmation
- Refund handling

AURELLE LIVING will not claim to accept a payment method until the relevant provider has been integrated and tested.

8. Material Samples

Verified reference observations

Danetti provides a free fabric swatch request service for eligible products.

Reference: https://www.danetti.com/pages/faqs

Potential AURELLE LIVING feature

A future material sample request feature may allow customers to request fabric or finish samples.

This feature depends on real sample inventory, fulfilment, and delivery arrangements. It is not included as a working service until those operations exist.

9. Trade Customer Experience

Research requirement

A premium furniture brand may need a separate enquiry journey for interior designers, property professionals, and trade customers.

Proposed AURELLE LIVING features

- Trade enquiry form
- Business details collection
- Project description
- Enquiry management for administrators
- Manual follow-up workflow

Trade discounts, dedicated account management, and business pricing must not be advertised until AURELLE LIVING has established those services.

10. Key Lessons for AURELLE LIVING

The reference research identifies these important design and business requirements:

1. Make furniture discovery simple.
2. Present product specifications clearly.
3. Make delivery information easy to find.
4. Explain returns and customer support clearly.
5. Keep the checkout process understandable.
6. Provide reliable order status information.
7. Separate genuine operational services from marketing claims.
8. Build a consistent experience across mobile and desktop.

11. Original Differentiation Strategy

AURELLE LIVING will develop its own identity through:

- Editorial luxury presentation
- Original photography or properly licensed imagery
- Distinctive typography and visual identity
- Curated product storytelling
- Detailed product specifications
- Thoughtful navigation
- Clear customer service information
- Responsive and accessible design

The final design will be original and will not reproduce Danetti's page layouts or brand identity.

12. Research Limitations

This document records public information observed on selected Danetti pages.

It does not establish:

- Danetti's internal technology architecture
- Its private database structure
- Its actual conversion rates or revenue
- Its internal business processes
- Its complete operational costs
- Its proprietary software or integrations

These matters have not been independently verified.
